Julius Caesar and Blaise de Vigenere stumbled upon a cryptic message: ERCH70GI4RHC_U4N_S3_73F10U5.
Blaise knew how to crack it but Julius was unable to figure it out.
Julius was aware of the way to crack it but he couldn't figure out the key.
So Blaise gave him a hint to get the key and asked him to use his own brain six times to get it and said "Multiples of 3 can be fun."


The hint given by Blaise: 
FNRYREVUPSTGQJW
The positions at which the characters of key are: 1 12 2 6 9 14 (not in the same word)
